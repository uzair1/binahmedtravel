Important Vaccination Update

Karachi Umrah passengers

All Umrah passengers travelling from Karachi Airport from 25th September onwards must have the following 3 vaccinations:

1. Polio
2. Meningitis
3. Influenza
Option 1
At Karachi Airport

Passengers can get the required vaccinations at the airport. Report at least 7 to 8 hours in advance to allow sufficient time for vaccination and other formalities.

Option 2
In Advance

Visit the International Travellers Vaccination Centre, Karachi to have your vaccinations done in advance.

Open in Google Maps

We strongly recommend getting the vaccinations completed in advance to avoid unnecessary delays or inconvenience at the airport.
